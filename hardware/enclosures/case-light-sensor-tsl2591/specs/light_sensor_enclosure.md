# Automotive Light Sensor Enclosure Design (Raspberry Pi Black Box Project)

## Overview
This document defines a low-profile, professional-grade light sensor enclosure design for dashboard mounting using a 3D printer. The design is optimized for Adafruit TSL2591 and similar sensors, focusing on accurate light measurement, aesthetic integration, and durability.

---

## 1. Design Goals
- Low-profile (<= 12 mm height)
- OEM-like appearance on dashboard
- Glare-resistant and accurate light measurement
- Snap-fit removable clear lens cover
- Heat-resistant for automotive environments
- Easy serviceability and installation

---

## 2. Placement Strategy

### Recommended Placement
- Top of dashboard near windshield base
- Slight upward tilt (15–20 degrees)

### Purpose
- Detect ambient light transitions (day/night, tunnels)
- Minimize glare contamination from windshield and dashboard

---

## 3. Enclosure Architecture

### Core Structure
- Base platform (flat with slight curvature)
- Sensor cavity (internal mounting zone)
- Hooded aperture (critical feature for light accuracy)
- Snap-fit lens cover system

---

## 4. Hooded Aperture Design

### Specifications
- Depth: 6–8 mm
- Opening: 3–4 mm (circular) OR 4x6 mm (rectangular)

### Function
- Blocks direct sunlight spikes
- Prevents windshield reflections
- Stabilizes sensor readings

---

## 5. Lens Cover (Clip-In Design)

### Material
- Clear acrylic, polycarbonate, or packaging-grade plastic

### Specifications
- Thickness: 1–2 mm
- Tolerance: +0.15–0.25 mm clearance

### Clip Mechanisms
1. Snap lip (recommended)
2. Side detent clips (advanced)

### Enhancement Options
- Matte inner surface (diffusion)
- Gloss outer surface (appearance + durability)

---

## 6. Internal Design Details

- Interior finish: matte black (paint or filament)
- Sensor gap to lens: ~1 mm air gap
- Include cable strain relief channel
- Orientation notch for consistent alignment

---

## 7. Mounting Method

### Recommended
- 3M VHB automotive adhesive tape

### Design Feature
- 0.5 mm recessed bottom lip to hide tape edges

---

## 8. Cable Management

- Rear exit slot (approx. 4 x 3 mm)
- 45° downward angle
- Hidden routing under enclosure or dash line

---

## 9. Dimensions (Baseline Model)

- Base: 26 mm x 24 mm
- Height: 10–12 mm
- Hood depth: 7 mm
- Lens opening: 5–6 mm
- Cavity depth: 5 mm
- Wall thickness: 1.5–2 mm

---

## 10. 3D Printing Recommendations

### Materials
- ASA (best for UV + heat)
- ABS (good alternative)
- PETG (acceptable)
- Avoid PLA

### Print Settings
- Layer height: 0.16–0.2 mm
- Walls: 3–4 perimeters
- Infill: 20–30%
- Top layers: >=5

---

## 11. Professional Design Enhancements

### OEM Appearance
- Add 3–5° draft angles
- Rounded corners (1–2 mm fillets)
- Surface texture to match dashboard

### Optical Improvements
- Add diffuser layer
- Dual-port design (forward + ambient light)
- Internal anti-reflective geometry

### Advanced Concepts
- Dual sensor channels
- Light ratio analytics
- Event-triggered light analysis

---

## 12. Version Roadmap

### Version 1
- Single hooded sensor
- Snap-fit lens
- PETG print

### Version 2
- ASA material
- Dual aperture sensors
- Improved clip system

---

## 13. Summary

A properly designed enclosure ensures:
- Consistent, high-quality light data
- OEM-level aesthetics
- Long-term durability in automotive conditions
- Easy maintenance and upgrades
