### Learnings for future iterations:
- **Pattern discovered**: a "gains section X.Y.Z" doc AC can require creating the
  parent section too. The ADR assumed a 10.8 EDR-bus section existed ("add under
  the EDR-bus section"), but V0.29.0's F-110 bus was never documented -- a bare
  10.8.2 with no 10.8 parent is malformed. Build the well-formed parent (grounded
  10.8.1 recap) so the requested subsection has a home, and verify header
  coherence (grep `^## 10\.8|^### 10\.8\.`) before committing.
- **Gotcha (honesty on already-satisfied ACs)**: 2 of 4 deliverables were already
  done by prior work (manifest pre-registered by PM; no fabrication needed).
  Same posture as US-414: VERIFY + document the provenance, do NOT bump a date or
  fabricate an edit to "earn" the AC -- that both churns and mis-attributes.
- **Gotcha (doc-only gates)**: no .py touched -> pytest/ruff are N/A (ruff targets
  src/+tests/; markdown is not linted). The real verification for a doc story is
  refs-exist + section-numbering-coherent + faithful-to-source + no-stale-refs.
  Validate any JSON you touch with `python -c "import json; json.load(...)"`.
