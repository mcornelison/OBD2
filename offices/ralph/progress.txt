### What was implemented:
- NEW SlowDrainDetector (src/pi/hardware/slow_drain_detector.py): a pure, hardware-free state machine over (timestamp, VCELL) samples emitting a battery-HEALTH DrainState verdict (UNKNOWN/STABLE/SLOW_DRAIN). A rolling window (default 300s) + net-decline threshold (0.005V) trip the RAW verdict; a debounce (30s) commits a verdict only after the raw signal holds its new value continuously for the debounce interval (flap suppression); a partial ramp-up window returns UNKNOWN (honest-instrument, never a confident STABLE).
