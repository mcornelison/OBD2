### Learnings for future iterations:
- **Pattern discovered**: To keep a `src/common/` DDL as the single source AND wire it into the Pi's
  existing table-creation loop, expose `*_SCHEMAS`/`*_INDEXES` `list[tuple[name, ddl]]` and
  `*`-splat them into `ALL_SCHEMAS`/`ALL_INDEXES`. The Pi inherits idempotency for free -- no
  bespoke create path, no second-boot risk.
- **Gotcha (import-root identity)**: `common.X` and `src.common.X` both resolve but are cached as
  DISTINCT module objects (dual import-root convention). A `foo is bar` identity check across the
  two roots ALWAYS fails; assert value equality (`==`) to prove "no divergent copy" instead.
- **Gotcha (index-in-ALL_INDEXES safety)**: a `drive_id` index is only safe in `ALL_INDEXES` when
  the column is defined in the table's own CREATE TABLE (true for the EDR tables). The US-200
  `realtime_data` drive_id indexes are deliberately NOT in `ALL_INDEXES` because that column was
  migration-added and may not exist when the indexer runs on a legacy DB.
- **Gotcha (env)**: mypy is not installed on this dev box; deferred to PM (module is fully hinted).
