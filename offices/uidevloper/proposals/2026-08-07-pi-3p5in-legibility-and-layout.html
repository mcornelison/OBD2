1 · The measurement

why 34 px, not "bigger"

Legibility is an angular requirement, not a pixel one — it depends on how far away you sit. The panel is 3.5″ diagonal at 480×320, which is ~165 PPI, so one design pixel is ~0.154 mm. At a normal in-car glance distance of ~650 mm:

RequirementCap heightDesign pxFont-size
Comfort target — 20 arcmin3.8 mm~24.5 px~34 px
Practical floor — 16 arcmin3.0 mm~20 px~28 px
Shipped primary values1.4–1.7 mm9–11 px13–15 px under
Shipped tile labels0.9–1.2 mm6–8 px8–11 px far under

Your report and the arithmetic agree — independently

You flagged the compass heading, the %-values, the g-force and the CHECK ENGINE line. Every one of those is ≤22 px. You did not flag the gear readout or the standby hero — and those are the only two elements on the whole UI at ≥34 px (.imu-gear 40 px, .idle-hero 34 px).

Two different methods landing on the same boundary is what makes 34 px a measured floor rather than my preference. It is your own eyes, in the car.

Type is an instrument too

A value rendered too small to read is not a degraded instrument — it is a silent one. It occupies the space of a working readout while telling you nothing, which is worse than an honest "no source", because it never announces its own failure.

What the "after" gave up, and where it went

The four detail lines (11 px) and the summary line are gone from the face. They were below the floor, so on the panel they were never read — they were consuming the space that made everything else too small. They move to the System drill-down, which already exists and is read parked, where 26 px is fine.

The card title also goes. With four tiles that each name themselves, "System Status" was labelling a screen you can already identify from its page dot.

4 · What the scale costs

and the screens it buys

Of the 320 px stage, the top bar (~30) and page dots (~16) leave ~258 px of card body. One label + value row at the new scale is 20 + 34 + gap ≈ 72 px. So:

LayoutFacts per cardVerdict
Single column3comfortable
2 × 2 grid4the ceiling
Anything denser5+back under the floor

This partly reverses the 6→4 consolidation — and it should

I designed that consolidation (F-124) at your request, and it was right for what you asked: too many screens to page through. But it is what packed the density that now costs legibility. On a 3.5″ panel, legibility outranks screen count — so the number goes back up.

Turning auto-rotate off (your disposition-B) makes that cheap: cards no longer advance under you, so paging is deliberate and a longer set costs you nothing but a swipe.

5 · Atlas asked what happens to the standby face

design-gate answer

He wants the live IMU instrument to be the Home face always, so that on the bench with the engine off you still see your compass and g-meter. His question back to me: what, if anything, remains of the idle/standby face?

It retires as a face, and survives as a state

Parked, the IMU is not unavailable — it is correct. The compass reads a true heading and the g-meter reads a true 0.0 g when you are stationary. Showing that parked isn't fabrication; it's the instrument working. There was never a good reason to hide it behind the word "standby".

What is genuinely absent parked is the OBD-dependent content — gear, and the speed-gated altitude integration. Those get typed-NA and greyed, which is the same honest-availability pattern Battery and Light already use.

Idle face carriedWhere it goesWhy
STANDBY heroDeletedReplaced by a live instrument showing a true parked reading. A real heading beats the word "standby".
ClockTop barIt is chrome, not a card fact.
"DTC not read since key-off"Alerts cardLoad-bearing honesty, and it was always Alerts' fact — idle was borrowing it. Alerts is now adjacent at position 2.

Net: no honest content is lost, one SSOT borrow is returned to its owner, and you get your compass on the bench.

6 · Knock-on to the Settings screen

F-126 · US-532

Atlas's F-126 design-gate ruling lands two changes on the Settings design I drew on 08-03:

RulingWhat my design does now
One key, not two. No new autoRotate bool alongside the existing autoRotateS.The toggle derives on/off from autoRotateS > 0; off writes 0, on writes the shipped default. Default is now OFF per your disposition-B.
Auto-rotate does not apply live — the config is read once at server startup and cached.That control is labelled "applies on restart". A silent no-op that looks applied is the one outcome the screen must never produce.
Power mode validates to {car, wall, unknown}.Anything else resolves to unknown — never a confident wrong mode.

The type scale reaches this surface too — Settings labels and values obey the §2 floor like everything else, which will shorten the list per screen.

7 · Before these values lock

on the panel, not the bench

The scale is derived, so it has to be confirmed against the real display at real distance. Two specific things to check:

1Read the set at arm's length in the car, seated normally — not leaning in at a desk. Desk-legible and car-legible are different tests, and only one of them counts.
2Check whether small text is also being resampled. The stage is authored at 480×320 and scaled up, with the Pi outputting 1080p into a 480×320 native panel. If the panel downsamples, small glyphs may be blurred as well as small — which would make the shipped 8–11 px worse than the arithmetic predicts. I have not confirmed this; it needs a look at the real hardware.
