3 · Design rules baked in

  • Honest idle, not fake-healthy. Idle shows STANDBY in neutral grey — never green "OK" (nothing was measured) and never amber/red (nothing is wrong). Green appears only on the battery line, which is a real recent reading carrying its "checked Nd ago" age.
  • Absence ≠ clean, absence ≠ fault. The Faults line reads "DTC not read since key-off" when there's no key-on read — not "No codes" (false all-clear) and not "Check Engine" (the phantom). A genuine stored STOP/WATCH still surfaces via the existing ribbon/takeover on every card.
  • Idle is derived from existing SSOT, not invented. Idle = system-status.source.obd.available===false AND drive.state==="idle". Display policy over facts the emitters already write — no new hardware polling (SSOT-design-pattern). When OBD wakes / a drive starts, the carousel auto-advances off idle to the live view.
  • Idle = the parked twin of the (EDR-gated) live home card (W-11). Same home slot: parked→this idle card; driving→the future live instrument. No separate drive-mode.
