The driving screen — what the glass shows in motion

The live-instrument home card: the driving twin of the shipped idle card, same carousel home slot. Compass heading, gear, road grade, and a g-force trail — read from the IMU feed, never fused on the display. When the feed goes quiet, it falls back to idle rather than fake motion.

The four instruments

Compass tape — heading from the magnetometer; scrolls under a fixed caret so a turn reads directionally.

Gear — Spool's OBD derivation. Shows -- when ambiguous (clutch-in, <5 km/h, ratio>15% off), never a wrong number.

Grade — pitch from the accel gravity vector + a ~15-min trend. Informational, no alarm.

G-force — lat/lon g with a fading ~35 s trail. Amber ring at 0.6 g (Spool) — doubles as an aged-tire load nudge. Never a takeover.

Honest fallback

Absent or stale states/imu → the home slot shows the idle card. No frozen or zeroed instrument pretending the car is stationary. Try the Stale toggle.

02

The contract — routes to Atlas (loop early)

DELTA-2 class · EDR-gated
  • Q-A · derived, not raw. The raw feed is accel/gyro/mag. The display needs display-ready headingDeg · gradePct · gLat · gLon. The reader derives these, the display never fuses sensors (same seam as the light bridge). Atlas confirm
  • Q-B · refresh rate. A compass tape + 35 s trail won't animate at the 1 Hz card poll — needs a higher-rate STREAM/SSE topic (Atlas's own DELTA-2 open item). Atlas confirm
  • Alarms live elsewhere. Coolant / knock / voltage / DTC-STOP ride the unified alert layer (DELTA-1) on top — a 🔴 is full-brightness + un-dismissable (Spool). This card stays calm; it never recolors into an alarm.
  • Gear is OBD, not IMU. Spool's derivation (validated vs Drive 30). The IMU may sharpen it later; the number stays his.
