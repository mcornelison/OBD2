Menu access — long-press vs ⋮

recommendation

Parked / idle — ⋮ visible (discoverable, safe)

BT⇅⚡V0.29.16⋮
STANDBY

The tension

⋮ is discoverable but a single tap to a consequential menu — easy to hit on a bumpy road. Long-press is deliberate but hidden.

A · Long-press onlycleanest + safest, least discoverable
B · Always ⋮shipped now; discoverable but a tap-in-motion risk
RECOMMENDEDC · Context-aware ⋮⋮ shows only when parked/idle; hidden while driving; long-press always works

Why

Same content + the exact same safety gates — just ordered so the eye lands on "what do I do" first.

Directive-first for 🔴/🟡: the action band sits right under the code, bigger.

Carded sections (context · fix · status · clear) group the wall into labeled blocks. Bigger ‹ Back.

Zero logic change — severity gating, the fix-replacement safety rule, the trust badge, and the Mode-04 clear gate are untouched.
