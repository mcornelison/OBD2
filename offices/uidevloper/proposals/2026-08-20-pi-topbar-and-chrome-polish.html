Two of your items are the same bug

The ⋮ hanging below the header and the text clipped off the bottom of the cards look unrelated. They aren't. F-127 raised the type scale and left every chrome band at its old height. The bars never grew to fit the type they now carry, and the card body never shrank on paper to match the space it actually lost.

The top bar is 28 px tall and holds 26 px glyphs plus a ⋮ set at 34 px inside a 40 px button. That is the first symptom. The second is arithmetic — below.

Three-column grid: glyphs left, clock centred on the bar, version + ⋮ right. Bar 28 → 34 px. ⋮ paints 28×28 and stays touchable at 42×40.

Why a grid and not two auto-margins. The existing code comment argues against centring the clock, and its reasoning is right as far as it goes — two auto margins in one flex row split the free space between them, so the clock would drift as the version string changed length. That's an objection to centring with auto-margins, not to centring. 1fr auto 1fr puts the clock at the bar's midpoint whether the chip reads V0.29.29, V0.29.100 or the honest V?.?.? sentinel. The drift becomes structurally impossible instead of merely avoided.

Why the ⋮ can shrink but the tap target can't. S-2 requires a ≥40 px touch target and the bar is smaller than that minimum, so the visual box and the hit box have to separate: the button paints at bar height, and a transparent pseudo-element extends the hit area past it. The glyph drops to the 26 px tier because a ⋮ is chrome, not a value — the 34 px driver-must-read floor was never meant to apply to it.

Same pattern, worth auditing in the same pass

Any control in a band shorter than the 40 px tap minimum needs this treatment. #menu-close and #sys-detail-back both carry min-height:40px inside 36 / 44 px headers.

Unreadable — and it's UTC, while the clock two bands up is local.

Proposed
SYNC OK Aug 17, 2026
7:30:28 PM
POWER CAR external

Local time, own line. 412 rows · 0 pending moves to the System drill-down — your call, 2026-08-20.

This is more than cosmetic. The top-bar clock renders local time; the sync stamp renders UTC. Side by side on one 3.5″ panel they can differ by hours and you have no way to tell which is lying. Formatting fixes the reading; converting to local fixes the contradiction.

  • One 12-hour rule, not two. The existing fmtClock comment already warns that two formatters is how one surface drifts back to 24-hour while every test of the other stays green. So both formatters call one shared helper.
  • A bad timestamp must not become a confident date. If the value won't parse, render the raw string — never Jan 01, 1970, never NaN. A fabricated date on the one tile whose job is to report whether the upload really happened is the green-when-broken failure exactly.
  • Absent stays never. Unchanged, and already right.

Once the contract lands
BTᯤ ⇅⚡ 7:42 PM V0.29.29 ⋮

P-1's grid absorbs the fourth glyph with no re-layout — the width check leaves ~132 px of slack against a 26 px glyph plus a gap.

Two findings for Atlas
  • It's a restoration, not an invention. The retired pygame screen already rendered WiFi status and SSID. The HTML migration dropped it — this is recovering a regression.
  • Don't conflate two facts. HomeNetworkDetector already exists, but it answers "is the Pi on the home WiFi" — not "is wlan0 associated." A glyph fed from home-detection would read DOWN every time the car is away from the house with a perfectly good link.
  • States up / weak / down / unknown. Absent or stale resolves to unknown, never a confident up — same rule the power tile follows. Existing tokens only; no new colour.
What happens next

P-1 through P-5 need no design gate — nothing changes shape in a state file, no emitter learns a new fact, no safety gate is touched. They're with Marcus to put in the backlog and schedule, as five stories. P-6 goes to Atlas first.

One constraint on the scheduling: P-1, P-2 and P-3 have to land in the same sprint. All three change the top bar or a band measured off it, and P-3 moves the bar height itself — so if they're split, a later story quietly invalidates an earlier one's sign-off. That's the same failure as the bug being fixed: a value changed in one place, a dependent measurement left behind. P-3 also promotes the band heights into tokens, because the root cause was 28px living as a literal in one file and an assumption in another.

The acceptance that counts is the same one F-127 still owes: read it in the car, at arm's length, seated normally. Item 3 shipped through a bench check because the arithmetic was wrong on paper and nothing on the bench contradicted it.
