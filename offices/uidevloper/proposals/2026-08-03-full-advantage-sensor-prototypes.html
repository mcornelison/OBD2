Eclipse OBD-II · UI/UX · sensor prototypes

Getting the most out of the sensors we already have

Four prototypes, no new hardware (GPS + baro on hold). Every readout is fact-checked against what the OBDLink actually returns on your DSM (MD326328) — and as of Spool's 08-07 probe ruling, BOOST IS OUT: MAP (0x0B) is probe-dead on this ECU and is wired to the EGR differential sensor, so it was never boost to begin with. MAF replaces it — the 2G 4G63 is MAF-based (Karman-vortex), so airflow is the real "how hard is it breathing" signal and the actual fuel-metering input. Nothing here shows a dead "no source" tile.

Killed by the 08-07 ruling — boost/MAP gauge (0x0B dead + wrong quantity), CONTROL_MODULE_VOLTAGE 0x42 (dead — voltage still shown, sourced from adapter ATRV), O2 gauge (narrowband slams 0.1–0.9 V by design — not AFR), timing gauge (base timing swings ±10–15°; reads as a knock gauge and isn't one). Boost is not displayable by any software fix — it needs a GM 3-bar + ECMLink, both behind the sensor freeze.

P1

Airflow + vitals on the driving view

folded → P2

What it adds

An airflow bar (MAF g/s) + a compact vitals row on the existing live card — the closest honest analogue to boost feel, plus temps/RPM/throttle at a glance.

MAF g/s coolant IAT RPM throttle — all Spool-confirmed GREEN.

Was a boost bar. MAP is dead + is the EGR differential sensor, so there is no boost term to render. MAF is the primary fuel-metering input on this engine — it rises with exactly the effort a driver is feeling.

IAT is labelled "INTAKE AIR", never "charge temp" — the 2G sensor sits in the AFM housing, pre-turbo and pre-intercooler. Advisory amber ~≥60 °C only; never red (IAT alone doesn't kill an engine, and we have neither boost nor knock to pair it with).

P2

Dedicated "Engine" card

★ priority

The full engine picture

A MAF arc gauge + RPM, coolant/IAT, throttle/load, fuel trims (STFT/LTFT), fuel-system state, voltage — one screen for the whole engine state.

Trade-off: adds a 5th card to the 4 you just consolidated. Good if you want engine depth on its own screen vs. folded into the driving view (P1).

Closed/Open Loop (FUEL_SYSTEM_STATUS 0x03) takes the tile O2 would have had — stable and honest, where a narrowband needle would just slam rail-to-rail.

Two constraints that shape this card, not decorate it:
1 · No smooth needles. The whole K-line budget is ~6.3 samples/sec across all PIDs — about one update per PID every 2.5 s, with no "turn up the Hz" knob. Animating between samples would fabricate values the ECU never sent. Everything here steps, and stale values show their age. Anything that must feel live comes off the IMU (100 Hz) or ATRV.
2 · Fuel trims are banded straight — no idle special-case. An earlier ruling had me suppress LTFT colour at idle, on a characteristic −6.25 % warm-idle lock. Spool withdrew that the same day: the figure came from the old ECU. Re-baselined against the current one (drives 25–38, n≈2,700) LTFT runs −3.9 % to +3.1 %, warm idle −2.6 % — inside the ±5 % green band, including the exact case the warning was about. No offset, no suppression, no branch.

P3

Post-drive review

★ priority

What the logged data unlocks

Every sensor is already logged to the server. This is the drive-summary: airflow/effort trace, g-force trace, corner-lean, grade profile — the "how did that drive go" view.

The 2.5 s sample rate doesn't hurt here. Post-drive is where the slow K-line stops mattering — a trace over 22 minutes reads fine at 0.4 Hz, and the IMU channels (g, lean, grade) are 100 Hz regardless. This surface gets more out of the existing sensors than any live gauge can.

Biggest build: server-tier surface (Atlas contract), not a Pi card — but the highest analytical value, and it's all data we already have.

Could live on the server dashboard or as an end-of-drive summary on the Pi.

P4

Attitude indicator — an aircraft gyro for the Eclipse

low priority · fun

The artificial horizon, for a car

The classic aviation "gyro horizon," mapped to the Eclipse: aircraft pitch → your road GRADE, aircraft bank → your body LEAN in corners. A round instrument face (clipped + bezel), a gradient sky/ground horizon that rolls with lean and pitches with grade, a pitch ladder, a fixed roll index + bank scale (10/20/30/45/60°) with a rolling amber pointer, and a 1998 Eclipse GST rear-view silhouette (traced from your reference — rounded body, narrowing hatch, the signature raised GST wing, twin exhaust) as your fixed reference: the car sits still while the horizon rolls behind it, so it visibly leans into corners — the view you'd have of your own car ahead.

Bottom of the face: the aviation slip/skid ball, repurposed as a lateral-g meter — it slides with cornering load, "step on the ball" for a car. All from confirmed IMU data.

IMU pitch → grade IMU roll → lean gyro yaw — all confirmed on the IMU.

Low priority + fun (your call). Needs the gyro/roll+pitch exposed in states/imu (Atlas/Ralph — small). The pitch ladder + roll scale are exaggerated slightly so gentle grades/leans still read.
